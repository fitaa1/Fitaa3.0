Questions

Straight answers.

Isn't it expensive?

Think of it as two bookings. If Get Chosen brings you two extra nights or four extra clients, it has paid for itself. And if it isn't live in 7 days, half comes back to you. Hosts: management companies typically take 15 to 25% of revenue every month; this is a one-time fee.

I already have Instagram.

Instagram is where people discover you. Your page is where they decide and book. It also puts you on Google, where people search when they're ready to buy, and your Instagram links straight to it.

Airbnb already brings me guests.

Keep Airbnb. This adds a direct line for repeat guests and referrals, where you keep the full rate.

Can you put my apartment on Google Maps?

Not a single rental apartment: Google doesn't allow Business Profiles for individual rentals, and we won't risk your listing. Guest houses with a reception and short-let companies with a staffed office can have one. For single units, your page shows the neighbourhood on a map and your name shows up in Google search.

Who else have you done this for?

You'd be one of our founding clients, which is why the price is lower and the guarantee is there. Our past work for brands in Accra is on the main site.
